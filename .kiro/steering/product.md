# Product Overview

focusaint is a habit and study management platform designed to build and maintain strong habit routines ,maximize user retention, learning outcomes, and engagement through gamified features, AI-driven personalization, and defensible value propositions.

## Core Value Proposition

Help users maximize learning outcomes and retention through:
- Interactive habit tracking with daily session logging
- Streak management to build consistency and habits
- AI-driven personalization and adaptive learning
- Analytics and insights for performance tracking
- Community features and peer accountability

## Target Users

Productivity enthusiasts, students, and lifelong learners who want to:
- Build consistent study habits
- Track their learning progress
- Maintain focus during study sessions
- Analyze their productivity patterns
- Engage with a learning community

## Business Model

### Free Tier
- Quick Mode (max 3 sessions/day)
- Daily limited LLM tokens
- Local device storage for study data
- Secure, non-deletable streak tracking
- Basic reminders, quizzes, and analytics
- Limited history and leaderboard access
- Single-device usage
- Community (read-only access)
- Ads and upgrade nudges

### Premium Tier
- Full cloud sync (notes, chats, sessions)
- Multi-device access
- Extended LLM token limits
- Custom session limits
- Unlimited Quick Mode & Deep Mode
- Unlimited revision scheduling
- Full history & advanced analytics
- Export notes (PDF/Markdown)
- Priority AI responses
- Custom AI persona
- Smart recommendations
- Private groups and advanced leaderboard
- No ads, backup & recovery, offline sync
- Early access to new features

## Moat Features (Defensibility)

These features create competitive advantages and lock-in:

- **Focus Score**: Behavior-based productivity metric unique to the platform
- **Adaptive Revision Engine**: AI-driven learning optimization that improves over time
- **Streak Insurance**: Ability to freeze streaks (premium feature)
- **Personalized AI Study Coach**: Memory-enabled AI that learns user patterns
- **Habit → Deep Conversion Path**: Intelligent tracking of user progression
- **Gamified Milestone Rewards**: Achievement system that drives engagement
- **Study Identity Profile**: Public badge system for social proof
- **Weekly Performance Reports**: Automated email insights
- **Peer Accountability Groups**: Social features for retention
- **Shallow Learning Detection**: AI intervention when patterns indicate ineffective study

## Business Goals

- Drive user upgrades through clear feature differentiation and defensible value
- Maximize retention with habit tracking, gamification, and adaptive learning
- Build community and social proof via leaderboards and public profiles
- Ensure data security and privacy for all users
- Create network effects through community and social features
